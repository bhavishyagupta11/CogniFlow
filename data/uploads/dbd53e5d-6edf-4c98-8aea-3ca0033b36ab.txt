Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of creating machines capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence.

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning.

Neural Networks consist of interconnected neurons organized into input, hidden, and output layers.

Large Language Models (LLMs) are trained using transformer architectures.

The Transformer architecture was introduced in the paper "Attention Is All You Need" by Vaswani et al. in 2017.

ChatGPT is based on the GPT family of transformer models.
